Privacy Policy

StillOnCall: WorkCall Tracker · Effective September 22, 2026
StillOnCall processes everything on your device. Nothing you see in this app is ever uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.

What this app does

StillOnCall: WorkCall Tracker ("the App", "we", "our") helps you see how much of your call activity — including calls made outside working hours — relates to work: calls with coworkers you tag, and optionally calls with numbers not saved as contacts (such as clients or vendors), if you turn that on in Settings.

Information we access

To do this, the App requests two Android permissions:

  • Call Log (READ_CALL_LOG) — phone numbers, call timestamps, durations, and call type (incoming/outgoing/missed) for calls on your device.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) — contact names and phone numbers, so you can choose which contacts count as your work team.

How this information is used

  • All call-log and contact data is read and processed entirely on your device.
  • The App matches call-log entries against the coworkers you've tagged (and, if enabled, unrecognized numbers) to compute daily/weekly/monthly statistics: call counts, talk time, and calls outside your configured working hours.
  • Your tagged team list, work-schedule settings, and computed statistics are stored locally in the App's own private database on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not transmit, upload, sync, or back up any of your call log, contacts, or app data to any server.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not record call audio, access SMS/messages, or track your location.
  • We do not create an account for you, and the App does not require sign-in.

Sharing you control

The App includes an optional Share feature and CSV export. These only run when you explicitly tap the Share or Export button, and only send the specific summary or file you chose — through Android's normal share sheet, to an app or recipient you pick. The App itself never initiates a share on its own.

Data retention and deletion

All App data lives in local app storage on your device. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data from Android Settings, permanently deletes everything the App has stored — there is nothing on our end to delete, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
